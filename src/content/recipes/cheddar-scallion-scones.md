---
title: "Cheddar Scallion Scones"
category: "main"
keywords: ["baking", "scones", "cheddar", "scallion"]
prepTime: 20
cookTime: 20
servings: 10
ingredients:
  - items:
      - name: "all purpose flour"
        qty: "2"
        unit: "cup"
      - name: "salt"
        qty: "1/2"
        unit: "tsp"
      - name: "baking powder"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "tbsp"
      - name: "butter, cold and cubed"
        qty: "6"
        unit: "tbsp"
      - name: "sharp cheddar cheese, grated"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "cup"
      - name: "scallions, chopped"
        qty: "4"
      - name: "eggs"
        qty: "2"
      - name: "sour cream"
        qty: "1/3"
        unit: "cup"
      - name: "dijon mustard"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "tbsp"
      - name: "hot sauce"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "tsp"
steps:
  - items:
      - "Grate the cheese."
      - "Chop the scallions, keeping both the white and green parts."
  - items:
      - "Preheat the oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper."
      - "In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking powder."
      - "Add in the butter and work in with a pastry blender or your fingers until the dough is crumbly."
      - "Add in the cheese and scallions."
      - "In a separate large bowl, mix together the eggs and sour cream. Add in the mustard and hot sauce."
      - "In the large bowl, add in the dry ingredients and stir until everything is evenly moistened and the dough is sticky."
      - "Flour a surface and put the dough on it in a 10\" x 2\" rectangle."
      - "Cut out triangles of scones."
      - "Place the scones on the baking sheet leaving an inch between them."
      - "Bake the scones for 20-23 minutes until they're golden brown."
      - "Let scones cool for about 10 minutes."
      - "Scones can be frozen and reheated in the oven for 20 minutes."
