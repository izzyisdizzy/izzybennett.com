---
title: "Cardamom Syrup"
category: "other"
keywords: ["syrup", "coffee", "matcha", "cardamom"]
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 20
servings: 12
tools:
  - "strainer"
ingredients:
  - items:
      - name: "cardamom pods"
        qty: "2"
        unit: "tsp"
      - name: "lemon peel"
        qty: "1"
      - name: "cinnamon stick"
        qty: "1"
      - name: "cassia cinnamon piece (optional)"
        qty: "2"
      - name: "vanilla extract"
        qty: "1/4"
        unit: "tsp"
      - name: "water"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "cup"
      - name: "demerara sugar"
        qty: "1"
        unit: "cup"
steps:
  - items:
      - "Toast the cardamom pods in a small pot for 1-2 minutes. Stir around the pods frequently."
      - "Add the cinnamon stick, cassia cinnamon pieces, lemon peel, vanilla extract, water, and sugar."
      - "Bring to a simmer."
      - "Let simmer 20 minutes."
      - "Take off heat and let fully cool."
      - "Strain the syrup and store in the fridge."
