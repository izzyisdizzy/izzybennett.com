---
title: "Fruit Syrup"
category: "other"
keywords: ["melon", "strawberry", "honeydew", "cantaloupe", "watermelon"]
prepTime: 5
cookTime: 0
servings: 12
ingredients:
  - items:
      - name: "container of cut fruit"
        qty: "1"
      - name: "granulated sugar"
        qty: "1"
steps:
  - items:
      - "In a container cover the fruit generously with sugar. It’s a 1:1 ratio fruit to sugar."
      - "Shake the container to mix the sugar and fruit."
      - "Let sit at room temperature for an hour."
      - "Shake the container again."
      - "Store in fridge for at least 8 hours up to 16 hours."
      - "Strain syrup into container and store in fridge."
notes:
  - "Some fruits that work well are melon, honeydew, strawberries, cantaloupe, watermelon, etc."
---
